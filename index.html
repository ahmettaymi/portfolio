What the data taught me

I compare hook pass, watch time, saves and shares across posts to decide what to keep, cut and test next.

Hook pass by Reel (100% − skip rate). Instagram didn't show a skip rate for every Reel.

Insight 01

Show the payoff in the very first second

My two best hooks — Nano Banana Pro (70.7%) and Kling 2.5 Turbo (61.0%) — open with a striking, concrete result (surreal AI images, a cinematic car shot) in the top half of a split screen, cutting roughly every second while I talk underneath.

Insight 02

Abstract openings lose people

Pomelli opened on dark, abstract launch footage. Only 19.4% passed the hook and average watch time was 4.8 s, the lowest in the set. Same format, same presenter; the only real difference was the first frame.

Insight 03

Reach comes from saves, not only the hook

Claude Skills had an average hook (50.2%) but became my biggest Reel: 400K views, 31.4K saves, 10.9K shares and 3.1K new followers. Practical, save-worthy content keeps getting pushed long after posting.

Next tests

What I'd iterate on

Result-first openings for every topic · on-screen promise vs. visual-only first frame · shorter intros on tutorial Reels · CTA placement after the first payoff instead of at the end.
